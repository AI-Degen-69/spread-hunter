## 1. Pre-Registered Decision Rule

Written and committed before any measurement was taken, so conclusions cannot be
reverse-engineered from post-hoc results.

### 1a. Hypotheses

1. **Rank Preservation:** `queue-clear-5m` again clears all three risk-shape gates
   (Sharpe ≥ 2.70, PF ≥ 2.50, MDD < $4.00) and leads the five on Sharpe — the #799
   edge was structural (stale-book refusal), not a one-day regime.
2. **Convergence:** the Sharpe/PF gaps between the five compress vs #799 while the
   order is preserved — current books differentiate the knobs less, but do not
   overturn them.
3. **Regime Change:** a different arm leads on risk shape (e.g. `balanced-mid` clears
   gates it failed in #799) — October's ranking did not survive the regime, and no
   arm inherits #799's candidacy without re-earning it.

### 1b. Statistical Power & Sample Size Gate

Per-trade σ and required N (Δ = $0.02) measured in #799
(`docs/audits/live-migration-799.md`, measured ten-arm table):

| Arm | σ (#799) | Required N ($0.02) |
|---|---|---|
| `queue-clear-5m` | 0.431 | 2,865 |
| `balanced-mid` | 0.849 | 11,148 |
| `loose-baseline` | 0.880 | 11,977 |
| `prudent-hybrid` | 0.774 | 9,261 |
| `static-floor-2c` | 0.835 | 10,778 |

At ~7.7 trades/hour/arm, a 24-hour rehearsal collects ~180–220 closes per arm —
below every arm's required N. Therefore:

- **Rule:** this run is **statistically underpowered** to resolve nominal PnL
  differences between arms. **No arm may be declared a profit winner.**
- If an arm's observed N clears its recomputed required N
  (`python scripts/stat_gate.py --dry-calc --sigma <observed σ>`), that arm alone
  may carry a PnL verdict; the rest are reported **INCONCLUSIVE on nominal PnL**
  and judged strictly on risk shape (Sharpe, profit factor, max drawdown, merge
  rate) plus the repo's statistical gate
  (`python -m scripts.reevaluate_tournament_run --run-dir <run dir> --verdict`).

### 1c. Separation Criteria ("Clearly Different")

An arm is "clearly different" from the #799 baseline for risk-adjusted metrics if:

1. **Sharpe proxy:** separates by at least 25% relative margin
   (|Δ| ≥ 0.25 × #799 value).
2. **Profit Factor:** separates by at least 0.30.
3. **Maximum Drawdown:** compresses drawdown by at least $1.50 or increases it by at
   least $1.50.
4. **Merge Rate:** separates by at least 10 percentage points.

### 1d. Mechanism Check (`queue_report.py`)

Using `python scripts/queue_report.py --db <db_path>` on the `queue-clear-5m` store:

- If ≥ 15% of quoted books cluster in the 4–6 minute window, the gate actively
  trims marginal books under current conditions, as in #799.
- If < 5% cluster there, the gate rarely binds now — its #799 edge came from a book
  distribution that no longer prevails.
