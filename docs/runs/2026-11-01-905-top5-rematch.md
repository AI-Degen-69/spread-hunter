## 1. Pre-Registered Decision Rule

Written and committed before any measurement was taken, so conclusions cannot be
reverse-engineered from post-hoc results.

### 1a. Hypotheses

1. **Rank Preservation:** `queue-clear-5m` again clears all three risk-shape gates
   (Sharpe ≥ 2.70, PF ≥ 2.50, MDD < $4.00) and leads the five on Sharpe — the #799
   edge was structural (stale-book refusal), not a one-day regime.
2. **Convergence:** the Sharpe/PF gaps between the five compress vs #799 while the
   order is preserved — current books differentiate the knobs less, but do not
   overturn them.
3. **Regime Change:** a different arm leads on risk shape (e.g. `balanced-mid` clears
   gates it failed in #799) — October's ranking did not survive the regime, and no
   arm inherits #799's candidacy without re-earning it.

### 1b. Per-KPI Sample Size Sufficiency Gate

No arm is compared against another arm. Each arm is measured independently:
every KPI unlocks on the observations **it** is estimated from, at 90/95/99
confidence (we strive for 95). Means rows (PnL expectancy ±$0.02, avg return
±2.0pp, stop-loss depth ±2.0pp) size from the arm's own measured stdev, so the
required N adapts to the arm; proportion rows (win / stop-loss / merge rate)
stay conservative (p = 0.50, ±5pt → 271 / 385 / 664 closes at 90 / 95 / 99);
stop-loss depth divides by stop exits only, durations by merges/stops only.

Per-trade σ measured in #799 (`docs/audits/live-migration-799.md`, measured
ten-arm table) shows why N adapts — noisier arms need more closes:

| Arm | σ (#799) |
|---|---|
| `queue-clear-5m` | 0.431 |
| `balanced-mid` | 0.849 |
| `loose-baseline` | 0.880 |
| `prudent-hybrid` | 0.774 |
| `static-floor-2c` | 0.835 |

At ~7.7 trades/hour/arm, a 24-hour rehearsal collects ~180–220 closes per arm.
At 95%, win / stop-loss / merge rates need 385 closes, so those verdicts stay
locked for every arm; a tight arm can still unlock PnL expectancy on its own
stdev while its stop-loss average stays gated. Therefore:

- **Rule:** each arm reports **per-KPI verdicts**. A KPI whose row reads
  SUFFICIENT at 95% may carry a verdict; any other KPI is reported
  **INCONCLUSIVE on that KPI** and the arm is judged on the KPIs that did
  unlock plus risk shape (Sharpe, profit factor, max drawdown, merge rate).
- **No arm-vs-arm profit-winner verdict exists.** The rank hypotheses in 1a
  are judged on risk shape, never on a nominal-PnL horse race between arms.
- Sufficiency is read off each arm's `sufficiency_rows` on the cockpit page
  (same builder as the `:8799` dashboard card), plus the repo's statistical gate
  (`python -m scripts.reevaluate_tournament_run --run-dir <run dir> --verdict`).

### 1c. Separation Criteria ("Clearly Different")

An arm is "clearly different" from the #799 baseline for risk-adjusted metrics if:

1. **Sharpe proxy:** separates by at least 25% relative margin
   (|Δ| ≥ 0.25 × #799 value).
2. **Profit Factor:** separates by at least 0.30.
3. **Maximum Drawdown:** compresses drawdown by at least $1.50 or increases it by at
   least $1.50.
4. **Merge Rate:** separates by at least 10 percentage points.

### 1d. Mechanism Check (`queue_report.py`)

Using `python scripts/queue_report.py --db <db_path>` on the `queue-clear-5m` store:

- If ≥ 15% of quoted books cluster in the 4–6 minute window, the gate actively
  trims marginal books under current conditions, as in #799.
- If < 5% cluster there, the gate rarely binds now — its #799 edge came from a book
  distribution that no longer prevails.
