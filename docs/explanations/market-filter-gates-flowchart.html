Spread Hunter Architecture

Market Filtering Gates: Regular vs Live Sports / eSports

Every 10 minutes (600s), scripts.filter_loop evaluates ~200 candidate binary markets from Polymarket Gamma to assemble runtime/markets.json. Live in-play sports and esports follow an optimized treatment path designed to capture volatile game volume while preventing bad fills.

Volume Admission Floor
$50,000 reg / $35,000 live
Live games get lower entry volume ($35k) via /events?live=true.
Volatility / Velocity Gate
Exempt for live
Live games won't be killed by wild price spikes during play.
Ranking Priority
Live Boosted
sort_eligible() puts volatility-exempt live sports at the very top.
Hard Safety Locks
Identical
Both lanes require 10¢-90¢ price band, ≥$500 depth, ≤0.02 spread.
🏛️ Lane 1: Regular Markets
POLITICS · MACRO · UPCOMING
Gate 1 · Discovery SCAN API
Gamma Paginated Discovery
Iterates top volume binaries via gamma-api.polymarket.com/markets until volume drops below threshold.
Threshold: 24h Vol ≥ $50,000 (Trial vs $125k)
Sub-floor volume rows (< $50k) are cheap-rejected before fetching books.
Gate 2 · Cheap Filters METADATA
Resolution & Settlement Check
Rejects already proposed or disputed outcomes to prevent resting maker quotes into settlement traps.
Rule: UMA Status ≠ "proposed" / "disputed"
Gate 3 · Identity TAXONOMY
Primary & Submarket Gate
Enforces Moneyline, Outright, Macro or Politics primary contracts. Rejects noisy player props and derivative lines.
Rule: No "Spread -3.5", "Total 48.5", "Over/Under"
Rejects derivative group labels and unhedgable multi-line props.
Gate 4 · Tape Movement CLOB TRADES
30-Minute Traded Notional
Queries CLOB data-api.polymarket.com/trades over last 1800 seconds. A dead tape cannot fill resting maker orders.
Threshold: Movement ≥ $200 / 30m
Also strictly checks range cents and velocity (min trades and last trade recency).
Gate 5 · Price Band CLOB BOOKS
Decided Outcome Boundary
Calculates mid-price (best_bid + best_ask) / 2 for YES and NO books. Outside the band, the market is already decided.
Boundary: 0.10 < Mid < 0.90
Gate 6 · Depth & Spread LIQUIDITY
Dual Book Structure Check
Ensures enough resting depth exists so our maker orders do not become adverse selection bait.
Thresholds: Top-3 Bid Depth ≥ $500 each · Spread ≤ 0.0205
Gate 7 · Horizon SETTLEMENT
Time to Expiry Window
Rejects far-off multi-year contracts that tie up capital indefinitely without turnaround.
Rule: Days to Resolve ≤ 30.0 Days
Gate 8 · Income & Ranking GRADUATION
Spread Income & Standard Ranking
Calculates expected revenue from tape movement scaled by book share. Ranked by baseline return_pct_day.
Status: Eligible → Added to runtime/markets.json
⚡ Lane 2: Live Sports & eSports
IN-PLAY · HIGH VOLATILITY · RAPID FILLS
Gate 1 · Discovery VENUE LIVE API
Direct In-Play Discovery
Scans Polymarket /events?live=true directly. Live games are admitted on a lower volume hurdle!
Lower Threshold: 24h Vol ≥ $35,000 (vs $50k reg)
Captures fresh games that started recently before 24h volume has had time to accumulate.
Gate 2 · Cheap Filters METADATA
Live Match State Check
Checks that game has not ended / settled. Uses venue declaration accepting_orders rather than game clocks.
Rule: accepting_orders = true & UMA unproposed
Gate 3 · Identity SERIES EXEMPTION
Match Winner & Moneyline Focus
Matches EPL, Champions League, NBA, CS2, LoL, Valorant match winners. Filters out micro-events (e.g. "Next goal in 5 min").
Exemption: Series title and event slug honored
Permits tournament playoffs & live series matches through strict whitelist.
Gate 4 · Tape Movement VOLATILITY EXEMPT
Live Tape & Volatility Exemption
Must measure ≥ $200 on tape, BUT is 100% exempt from range and velocity gate rejection!
Exemption: is_volatility_exempt = true
Normal markets are rejected if price jumps wildly; live games EXPECT price action as teams score!
Gate 5 · Price Band SAFETY LOCK
Decided Outcome Boundary
Same hard safety rule: once a team is 95% to win (blowout), mid leaves 0.10-0.90 and quoting stops.
Strict Lock: 0.10 < Mid < 0.90 (No exemption!)
Gate 6 · Depth & Spread SAFETY LOCK
Dual Book Structure Check
Must have ≥ $500 top-3 bid depth and tight spread (≤ 0.0205) so paired buying remains profitable.
Strict Lock: Depth ≥ $500 · Spread ≤ 0.0205
Gate 7 · Horizon SAME DAY
Immediate Settlement Horizon
Live games almost always resolve within a few hours (< 0.5 days), passing the 30-day limit trivially.
Status: Days to Resolve ≈ 0.05d - 0.25d
Gate 8 · Income & Ranking PRIORITY BOOST
Live Sports Priority Boost
In sort_eligible(), volatility-exempt live sports are ranked before all regular markets!
Boost: Ranked FIRST → Instant Quoting Priority
Captures live high-frequency taker fills before match concludes and settles!
