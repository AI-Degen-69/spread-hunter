Shadow run, start to finish

One process, one loop. It reads the same market list and prices as the live bot, decides with the same rules, and only pretends at the two places where money would move: placing orders and merging. Every number below was read from the code.

The quote gates — if / then

IfThen
No two-sided book, or price off the 0–1 scaleSkip market. Nothing to rest against.
Less than 15s left to resolveSkip. No time to be filled fairly.
More than 40% into the trading windowSkip. Late quotes rest into convergence.
25 fills already on this marketStop quoting it. Enough exposure.
Spent ≥ $400 here and balanced (≥0.92)Stop quoting it. Cap reached, hedge complete.
Spent ≥ $400 but lopsidedLight side only. Buying the light side reduces exposure.
Fresh market (0 fills): both sides quotable at touch and UP + DOWN < $0.99Quote both at ask − 1¢.
Fresh market but only one side quotable, or pair ≥ $0.99Sit out. A lone leg is a bet nobody approved.
Quote would sit > 15¢ from mid (the engine default — BOT_QUOTE_BOOK_MAX_SPREAD_FROM_MID can widen it), or outside band 0.10–0.90Skip that side. Too far from mid to fill, or settled-outcome pricing.
Naked leg and fill cost + opposing ask < $0.99Complete the pair (buy the light side).
Naked leg but completion would cost ≥ $0.99, or no opposing ask at allUnwind the leg (exit). Never hold on hope.
Both legs matched, equal sizeMerge → +$1.00 of collateral (paper in shadow).

Intervals and triggers

Market scan → filefilter loop rewrites the list about every 10 min
Rotation cadencewake at most every 1s; a full pass costs ~45–60s of public reads
Universe freshnesslist older than 24h is refused, not traded
Pair sweepevery rotation, after settling and before quoting
Heartbeatrewritten every rotation; crash = stale file = honest STOPPED
Finish--minutes budget, finish-line trade/merge counts, Ctrl+C, or STOP
Uncertainty, stated plainly: fill crediting assumes tape prints it can see are the whole story — a fill that happens off-tape view is missed, and queue-ahead is modelled, not measured. That is why shadow results read as optimistic-ish, never as promises.
