01 The 3 Critical Upgrades Applied

1. Lone-Maker Unquoted Opposite Token Resolution
BUG FIX

In Underdog mode (BOT_QUOTE_REQUIRE_TWO_SIDED_WHEN_FLAT_ON = 0), the engine only bids on contracts ≤ 50¢. Previously, because the opposite side was never quoted, the stop-loss engine threw PairExitRefused("missing_side_tokens") and completely refused to sell falling inventory, causing 5 positions to expire to $0.00 (-100% loss).

Before (Round 1 Flaw)

Order manager looks for both UP and DOWN tokens in the quotes ledger.

  • Opposite side never quoted → side_tokens missing one leg.
  • Engine marks trade as STRANDED_MISSING_SIDE.
  • Stop-loss completely aborted; no sell order submitted.
  • 5 positions held into resolution → -$9.72 booked loss (-100%).
Now (Engine Upgraded)

Engine recognizes lone-maker mode and resolves the opposite side slot dynamically.

  • Position evaluation proceeds immediately on the held token.
  • When bid drops past -20%, exit_single_buy fires market sell.
  • Position liquidated cleanly at ~80% of entry value.
  • Saves ~$1.50 to $2.20 per trade on catastrophic drops.
2. Trailing Rider Breakeven & Activation Floor
ALGORITHMIC LOGIC

Previously, the trailing rider activated the moment held_bid > held_average (even by 1¢). On a 5¢ or 8¢ trailing delta, a pullback from a 1¢ noise tick triggered trailing_rider_exit well below the purchase price, turning 60%–70% of trailing exits into losses.

Before (Noise Triggered Loss)

Entry at 50¢, delta 5¢. Price ticks to 51¢ (+1¢ noise).

  • Peak set to 51¢. Trailing threshold: 51¢ - 5¢ = 46¢.
  • Price drops to 46¢ → Trailing exit triggers!
  • Position closed at 46¢ (-4¢ / -8% loss).
  • A tool meant to "ride winners" prematurely took losses!
Now (Profit-Guarded Rider)

Trailing exit requires peak ≥ entry + delta (e.g. 50¢ + 5¢ = 55¢).

  • Sub-delta noise ticks (51¢–54¢) do NOT arm the exit.
  • Threshold is mathematically clamped at or above entry.
  • Trailing rider threshold only arms at or above entry cost (locking in runner gains).
  • Adverse drops below entry are handled exclusively by the hard stop.

Key Mathematical Result: The trailing rider is now strictly asymmetric — it participates fully in upside runs (locking in profits on pullbacks from true peaks) while refusing to execute exits at a loss during early consolidation.

2. Shift to True Asymmetric Win/Loss PnL

Comparing favorite arms vs underdog arms demonstrates why Underdog arms (≤ 50¢) have an overwhelming structural advantage:

  • Favorites (0.60 ceiling): Max possible gain is ~40¢, while a 20% stop risks 12¢. PnL ratio is at best ~3:1.
  • Underdogs (0.50 ceiling): Entry at 15¢ with 20% stop risks only 3¢, but a win pays out 85¢ (a 28:1 reward-to-risk ratio!).

We expect live-ud50-r5c-stop20pct to break out strongly positive (targeting +$8.00 to +$15.00+ on similar volume), while live-rider-2c-stop30pct will likely continue lagging due to noise shakeouts.
