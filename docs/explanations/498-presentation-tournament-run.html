Pairs stay together

Every trade is two halves bought for less than $1 combined. The system now welds the halves under one ID, cancels hopeless strays, and never runs the same job twice — so no half-trade is left behind silently.

Honest labels

A missing half now says exactly what happened: green when the other half already paid out, requoting when it was cancelled, waiting when nothing happened yet. No more guessing from a blank cell.
