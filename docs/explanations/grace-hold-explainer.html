4. Why it was removed (The #622 Decision)

Holding quotes through book flicker preserved queue position, but holding during adverse drift created unhedged exposure when orders filled while the opposite leg was refused. In #622 the operator chose Option A (TOTAL PURGE): eliminate all hold and grace machinery entirely. Orders rest while quotable and cancel immediately when not quoted.
