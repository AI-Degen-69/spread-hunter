# Noticed-but-not-touching — Issue #515 (Station III)

Issue #515 adds two pure functions and their tests, plus the `queue_realism` payload key and
the `#queue-realism-readout` card on the Performance & Analytics tab. It changes no writer
and no separate reader in the registry-ingestion path, so the missing writer described below
was not fixed here. The first entry is the one worth the operator's attention: it decides
whether these metrics can ever stop being hand-fed.

| ID | Candidate | Discovering station | Evidence | Status | Resolution |
|----|-----------|---------------------|----------|--------|------------|
| N1 | **Nothing persists the clearing print, so the depth metric has no rows on any real run.** (The field's consequence was narrowed by the operator-approved readout in this same branch: `#queue-realism-readout` now renders the metric live, the latency half measures real waits from `quotes.ts` + `fills.venue_ts`, and the depth half reads `prints not recorded N` until a writer lands.) The trade-through rule proves the level cleared by a print strictly below our price, but the credit path keeps only our own price and the settlement time: `credit_fills` returns `ShadowFill(local_id, token_id, o.price, o.remaining)`, and `_credit_market` writes `FillRecord(price=f.price, venue_ts=now_ms, recorded_ts=now_ms)`. The strictly-below prints live only in the local `traded` dict and are dropped when the function returns. `queue_marks` cannot stand in for them either: it records tape volume at the level we rest at, never below it. So `trade_through_depth` measures correctly on any record it is handed and reads zero-count on every real run until a writer records the closest below-print at credit time — an additive telemetry write next to `queue_marks`, not a fill-model change, since it would alter nothing about which orders fill. #515's own scope excludes it ("DB and tape ingestion are out of scope", operator-approved), which is why it is recorded here instead of built. | III | `core_brain/shadow_fills.py:135-146` (`ShadowFill(o.local_id, o.token_id, o.price, o.remaining)`); `core_brain/shadow_exec.py:678-695` (`now_ms = int(now_fn() * 1000)`, then `FillRecord(..., price=f.price, venue_ts=now_ms, recorded_ts=now_ms)`); `core_brain/shadow_exec.py:143-159` (`queue_marks` schema: `price`, `level_size`, `traded`, `cancel_decay`) | open | Not filed. Needs the operator's call: capture the closest strictly-below print per credited fill (new column on `fills` or a small table beside `queue_marks`), then `cleared_fills` reads it instead of leaving `clearing_price` as `None`. |
| N2 | **A shadow fill's timestamp is the cycle that recognised the trade-through, not the print's own time.** Both `venue_ts` and `recorded_ts` are set from one `now_ms` per credit pass, so a wait measured from these fields is an upper bound on the real rest, and two fills credited in the same cycle can carry identical waits. The tape's own print time is not available anywhere in the shadow path, so there is no field to substitute. | III | `core_brain/shadow_exec.py:678-695` (one `now_ms` for every fill in the pass) | dismissed | Documented rather than changed: stated in `ClearedFill`'s field mapping and in `fill_latency`'s docstring. Recovering the print time would mean carrying tape timestamps through the credit path — a fill-model change, explicitly out of #515's scope (`shadow_fills.credit_fills`, `shadow_exec`). |
| N3 | **`kpi.median_seconds_to_fill` clamps a negative wait to `0.0`; `fill_latency` excludes and counts it instead.** Two readers can therefore report different wait samples on the same store: a settlement artefact becomes an "instant fill" in the KPI payload and an `excluded` row in the realism payload. | III | `core_brain/kpi.py:1449` (`sec_to_fill = max(0.0, (float(f["venue_ts"]) / 1000.0) - float(q["ts"]))`) vs `statistical_analytics.fill_latency` | dismissed | Deliberate, and documented in `fill_latency`'s docstring: `0.0` is a claim that a fill was instant, and the row cannot support that claim. `kpi.py` is out of this ticket's scope (its payload is read by the dashboard), so the divergence is recorded, not reconciled. |
| N4 | **Milliseconds and seconds are mixed across the registry, so any future ingestion ticket must convert in exactly one place.** `orders.posted_ts`, `fills.venue_ts`, `fills.recorded_ts` and `quotes.fill_ts` are milliseconds (`_attribute_fill_to_quote` writes `MIN(COALESCE(venue_ts, recorded_ts))`); `quotes.ts`, `queue_marks.ts`, `markouts.ts` and `closes.ts` are seconds. `kpi.py` already carries the conversion inline, which is how a second reader ends up carrying a second copy of it. | III | `core_brain/order_registry.py:1210-1238` (`_attribute_fill_to_quote`); `core_brain/kpi.py:1447-1449`; `ClearedFill`'s field mapping in `core_brain/statistical_analytics.py` | open | Recorded for the N1 follow-up: build `ClearedFill` rows in one builder that converts ms → s once, so no second reader re-derives the factor. #515 receives seconds and never converts. |

Nothing here needs a change on this branch beyond what the readout already does: N1's depth
half is the missing writer, and N4 is the unit conversion `cleared_fills` now performs once.
N2 and N3 are documented decisions, each with its reason attached where a reader meets the
number.
