The rule, in three columns

Left · the pill
Is it on or off?
A coloured light plus the status: running, stopped, down, or unknown. That's all it says now.
Middle · the name
Which service is this?
The full, readable name. Hover it for the longer description if you want the detail.
Right · the extras
Anything worth knowing?
Real detail only — how long it's been up, how many alerts, or a practice run that finished. Empty when there's nothing to say.

Try it yourself

  1. Open the dashboard, then click the SERVICES pill in the top bar. → the services dropdown opens.
  2. Look at any row. → the left pill is just a dot and a status word — no repeated name. The middle is the full service name; the right side shows extra detail or is blank.
  3. Compare two rows — one running, one stopped. → each pill carries its own status, and none of them echo "stopped" in the right column.
