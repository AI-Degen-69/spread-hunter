Ladder pricing · change #343

Our buy prices now follow the real market, not a guess

Before, every price level in the ladder sat around 50¢ no matter what the market was actually doing — so on a market trading at 68¢ we were offering far below where anyone was buying. Now each level rests at the live best price and steps down one tick at a time. And if a market is too expensive to ever complete safely, we post nothing at all instead of posting a price that can only lose money.

Drag the two prices and watch the ladder

UP and DOWN are the two sides of the same bet. Each level is a buy offer on one side. Green = we can pair it safely under 99¢. Red = pairing it would lose money, so it is never posted.

What this means for you

Live
Levels price off the real market, so a lopsided market gets a lopsided ladder instead of a flat one.
0
Quotes are placed on a market that can never complete under the 99¢ cap. Nothing posted means nothing can be lost.
