A market you hold now tells you whether the engine is working it

Before, a market you own looked identical whether the strategy was busy on it or had walked away. You had to leave the table you were reading and cross-check another screen. Now the answer sits next to the market name.

ORDERS & TRADES

What you get now

A green RESTING or QUOTING pill sits beside every held market that the engine is working on, with the same blinking dot you already know from Active Markets.

A market nobody is working gets nothing at all — no grey label, no new column, no empty space. Nothing there means nothing happening.

The two tabs read the verdict from one shared rule, so they cannot give you two different answers about the same market.

Check it yourself in the real app

  1. Open http://127.0.0.1:8801 in your browser.
  2. Press ORDERS & TRADES → Open Positions.
  3. Look beside each market name: a green pill. One market says QUOTING, the rest say RESTING.
  4. Press Active Markets and find the same four markets — the words must match market for market.
  5. Go back to Open Positions and wait a few seconds: CURRENT PRICE, MARK VALUE and UNREALIZED P&L keep moving, the pill stays put.
  6. Switch your computer to reduced motion and reload: the pill keeps its colour, the dot stops blinking.
