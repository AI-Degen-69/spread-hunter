1. System Loops & Execution Intervals

Loop Cadences

The system runs multiple asynchronous loops with dedicated execution frequencies to balance venue rate limits, order latency, and book freshness.

EVERY 10 MIN (600s)

🔍 Market Filter Loop

scripts/filter_loop.py queries the venue, evaluates ~200 candidate markets for depth > 200sh and spread < 6¢, and writes survivors to runtime/markets.json.

EVERY 1.0 SECONDS

⚡ Fleet Trader Loop

core_brain/trader_loop.py performs order reconciliation, fetches live order books, evaluates pricing & risk gates, plans submits/cancels, and sweeps the float.

15-MIN ACTION WINDOW

🚑 Single-Buy Rescue

core_brain/single_buy_saver.py acts on one-sided fills within 15 minutes of last_fill_ts to complete the pair or execute an unhedged stop-loss.

HORIZONS: 5m, 15m, 1h, 6h

📊 Post-Fill Markout Horizons

Fill mark-to-market prices are tracked at (300s, 900s, 3600s, 21600s) to measure toxic flow and trigger posture widening if adverse drift exceeds -0.5¢/share.

EVERY 2.0 SECONDS

🖥️ Dashboard Telemetry

dashboard/server.py polls data/orders.db every 2000ms with a 500ms SSE event stream to render live PnL, active orders, and reconcile lag.

2. `plan_orders` Re-Quote & Timing Mechanics

Queue Priority Protection

Does `plan_orders` re-quote on a fixed timer?
No. Re-quoting is price-event driven with a strict hysteresis window (Dead Band) to protect maker queue seniority.

3¢ Re-Quote Dead Band (`requote_dead_band = 0.03`)

Every cancel+replace sends an order to the back of the queue (median queue depth ahead is ~1058 shares). The 3¢ dead band suppresses ~48% of unnecessary re-quotes caused by minor mid-price flutter, giving orders time to fill.

1.0s Cycle Re-Gating

Kept orders are re-tested against risk.completable_pair_block every single second. If the opposing token's ask price increases such that the pair can no longer complete profitably, the order is cancelled immediately.

3. Core Brain Risk Parameters

Configured in core_brain/
Parameter Name Default Value Enforcement Location Operational Role & Protection
max_completable_pair_cost 1.00 ($1.00) core_brain/risk.py Completable-Cost Gate: Refuses any maker bid if bid + best_ask(other_leg) ≥ 1.00. Guarantees a single fill can be hedged at market ask without loss.
max_pair_cost 0.99 ($0.99) core_brain/config.py Profit Condition: Hard ceiling on combined both-maker bids (up_bid + down_bid) to ensure net merge profit.
max_naked_usd 6% of portfolio ($5.40 at $90, $6.00 at $100) core_brain/risk.py Dynamic Directional Cap: 6% of account value. Denominator for quadratic size ladder & inventory skew spring.
MAX_ORDER_USD 25% of portfolio ($22.50 at $90, $25.00 at $100) core_brain/venue.py Dynamic Single Order Ceiling: 25% of account value. Caps single order notional.
MAX_TOTAL_USD 90% of portfolio ($81.00 at $90, $90.00 at $100) core_brain/venue.py Dynamic Bankroll Ceiling: 90% of total portfolio value deployed across active orders and inventory.
max_skew 0.015 (1.5¢) core_brain/risk.py Inventory Skew Spring: Pushes heavy-leg bid outward and pulls light-leg bid closer to mid to accelerate flattening.
emergency_hedge_frac 0.8 (80%) core_brain/quotes.py Taker Cross Trigger: At 80% of max_naked_usd, permits the light side to cross the spread and take the hedge before cap freezes.
max_book_spread 0.06 (6¢) core_brain/config.py Book Health Spread Gate: Rejects books with bid-ask spread wider than 6¢ to prevent quoting into illiquid spreads.
min_book_depth_sh 200.0 shares core_brain/config.py Book Depth Floor: Rejects markets with less than 200 shares in top-3 levels to guarantee exit liquidity.
decided_price 0.02 (2¢ / 98¢) core_brain/config.py Decided Outcome Gate: Refuses to quote markets priced ≤ 0.02 or ≥ 0.98 where outcome is resolved.
