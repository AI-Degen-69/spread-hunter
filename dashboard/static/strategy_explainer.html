1. System Loops & Execution Intervals

Loop Cadences

The system runs multiple asynchronous loops with dedicated execution frequencies to balance venue rate limits, order latency, and book freshness.

EVERY 10 MIN (600s)

🔍 Market Filter Loop

scripts/filter_loop.py queries the venue, evaluates ~200 candidate markets for depth > 200sh and spread ≤ 2.05¢ (0.0205), and writes survivors to runtime/markets.json.

EVERY 1.0 SECONDS

⚡ Fleet Trader Loop

core_brain/trader_loop.py performs order reconciliation, fetches live order books, evaluates pricing & risk gates, plans submits/cancels, and sweeps the float.

15-MIN ACTION WINDOW

🚑 Single-Buy Rescue

core_brain/single_buy_saver.py acts on one-sided fills within 15 minutes of last_fill_ts to complete the pair or execute an unhedged stop-loss.

HORIZONS: 5m, 15m, 1h, 6h

📊 Post-Fill Markout Horizons

Fill mark-to-market prices are tracked at (300s, 900s, 3600s, 21600s) to measure toxic flow and trigger posture widening if adverse drift exceeds -0.5¢/share.

EVERY 2.0 SECONDS

🖥️ Dashboard Telemetry

dashboard/server.py polls data/orders.db every 2000ms with a 500ms SSE event stream to render live PnL, active orders, and reconcile lag.

2. `plan_orders` Re-Quote & Timing Mechanics

Queue Priority Protection

Does `plan_orders` re-quote on a fixed timer?
No. Re-quoting is price-event driven with a strict hysteresis window (Dead Band) to protect maker queue seniority.

3¢ Re-Quote Dead Band (`requote_dead_band = 0.03`)

Every cancel+replace sends an order to the back of the queue (median queue depth ahead is ~1058 shares). The 3¢ dead band suppresses ~48% of unnecessary re-quotes caused by minor mid-price flutter, giving orders time to fill.

Judged Once, at Placement

A resting order is never re-tested against the live hedge ask: a pair's economics are its rest prices, so a moving opposite ask cannot cancel it. The entry gates (quotes.dynamic_pair_sum, risk.hard_block) judge new bids only, before they are placed.

3. Core Brain Risk Parameters

Configured in core_brain/
Parameter Name Default Value Enforcement Location Operational Role & Protection
max_completable_pair_cost [USD] 1.00 ($1.00) core_brain/risk.py Maker Scope Completable-Cost Gate: Refuses any maker bid if bid + best_ask(other_leg) ≥ 1.00. Guarantees a single fill can be hedged at market ask without loss.
max_pair_cost [USD] 0.99 ($0.99) core_brain/config.py Maker Scope Profit Condition: Hard ceiling on combined both-maker bids (up_bid + down_bid) to ensure net merge profit.
max_naked_usd [USD] 6% of portfolio ($5.40 at $90, $6.00 at $100) core_brain/risk.py Maker Scope Dynamic Directional Cap: Maker risk budget (6% of account value). Denominator for quadratic size ladder & inventory skew spring; hard block halts quoting when exceeded.
max_order_usd [USD] 25% of portfolio ($22.50 at $90, $25.00 at $100) core_brain/venue.py Venue Scope Dynamic Single Order Ceiling: 25% of account value ($1.00 minimum floor). Enforced per-order via order_caps() and derive_dynamic_caps across 3 order managers/enforcers to prevent single-fill blowout.
max_total_usd [USD] 90% of portfolio ($81.00 at $90, $90.00 at $100) core_brain/venue.py Venue Scope Dynamic Bankroll Ceiling: 90% of total portfolio value deployed across all open orders and unmerged inventory. Enforced at venue level.
max_skew_offset [cents Offset] 0.015 (1.5¢) core_brain/risk.py Maker Scope Inventory Skew Spring: Pushes heavy-leg bid outward and pulls light-leg bid closer to mid by up to 1.5¢ offset to accelerate inventory flattening.
emergency_hedge_pct [% Ratio 0..1] 0.8 (80%) core_brain/quotes.py Maker Scope Taker Cross Trigger: At 80% (0.80 ratio) of max_naked_usd, permits the light side to cross the spread and take the hedge before cap freezes.
max_book_spread [cents Offset] 0.06 (6¢) core_brain/config.py Maker Scope Book Health Spread Gate: Rejects books with bid-ask spread wider than 6¢ to prevent quoting into illiquid spreads.
min_book_depth_shares [Shares] 200.0 shares core_brain/config.py Maker Scope Book Depth Floor: Rejects markets with less than 200 shares in top-3 levels to guarantee exit liquidity.
decided_price [USD] 0.02 (2¢ / 98¢) core_brain/config.py Maker Scope Decided Outcome Gate: Refuses to quote markets priced ≤ 0.02 or ≥ 0.98 where outcome is resolved.
