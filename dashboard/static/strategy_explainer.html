1. System Loops & Execution Intervals

Loop Cadences

The system runs multiple asynchronous loops with dedicated execution frequencies to balance venue rate limits, order latency, and book freshness.

EVERY 10 MIN (600s)

🔍 Market Filter Loop

scripts/filter_loop.py queries the venue, evaluates ~200 candidate markets for depth > 200sh and spread ≤ 2.05¢ (0.0205), and writes survivors to runtime/markets.json.

EVERY 1.0 SECONDS

⚡ Fleet Trader Loop

core_brain/trader_loop.py performs order reconciliation, fetches live order books, evaluates pricing & risk gates, plans submits/cancels, and sweeps the float.

15-MIN ACTION WINDOW

🚑 Single-Buy Rescue

core_brain/single_buy_saver.py acts on one-sided fills within 15 minutes of last_fill_ts to complete the pair or execute an unhedged stop-loss.

HORIZONS: 5m, 15m, 1h, 6h

📊 Post-Fill Markout Horizons

Fill mark-to-market prices are tracked at (300s, 900s, 3600s, 21600s) to measure toxic flow and trigger posture widening if adverse drift exceeds -0.5¢/share.

EVERY 2.0 SECONDS

🖥️ Dashboard Telemetry

dashboard/server.py polls data/orders.db every 2000ms with a 500ms SSE event stream to render live PnL, active orders, and reconcile lag.

2. `plan_orders` Re-Quote & Timing Mechanics

Queue Priority Protection

Does `plan_orders` re-quote on a fixed timer?
No. Re-quoting is price-event driven with a strict hysteresis window (Dead Band) to protect maker queue seniority.

3¢ Re-Quote Dead Band (`bot_order_requote_dead_band_cents = 0.03`)

Every cancel+replace sends an order to the back of the queue (median queue depth ahead is ~1058 shares). The 3¢ dead band suppresses ~48% of unnecessary re-quotes caused by minor mid-price flutter, giving orders time to fill.

Judged Once, at Placement

A resting order is never re-tested against the live hedge ask: a pair's economics are its rest prices, so a moving opposite ask cannot cancel it. The entry gates (quotes.dynamic_pair_sum, risk.hard_block) judge new bids only, before they are placed.

3. Canonical Strategy & Screener Parameters

Single-Source Config (core_brain & scoring)

Variables are organized into strict canonical scopes. The Bot Engine runs the exact same parameters whether in Live Mode (Real Trading) or Shadow Mode (Paper Trading); the only distinction is the execution adapter (signed live orders vs. paper fill simulator). All profit derives strictly from pair-assembly merge arbitrage ($1.00 - pair cost); graduated spread markets carry $0.00 maker rewards and rebate accrual is disabled.
